Frequently Asked Questions

Don’t see your question? Call 000 000-0000.

What services does Cartronix Auto Works offer?

Tires, wheels, general auto repair, brakes, oil changes, alignments, suspension service, lift kits, vehicle upgrades, window tint, automotive glass, and select Tesla services.

Not sure if we offer what you need? Give us a call.

Do I need an appointment?

Availability depends on the service. Call ahead and we’ll help you find the best time to bring your vehicle in.

Can you help me choose tires or wheels?

Yes. Tell us what you drive and what you want from your tires or wheels, and we’ll walk you through the options.

Do you work on customized trucks and SUVs?

Yes. We offer wheels, tires, suspension services, lift kits, accessories, and other upgrades.

Do you service Teslas?

We provide select Tesla services including tires, alignments, brake service, ceramic tint, and lowering kits.

Can I request a quote online?

Yes. Send the Request a Quote form with your vehicle information and the service you need.
